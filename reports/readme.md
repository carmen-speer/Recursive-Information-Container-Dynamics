# Reports

This folder holds the narrative, findings, and process record of how the
higher-education collapse tracker was actually built — the account of what
was done, not just the code that does it.

## Contents

- [`RICD Tracker Findings Final.pdf`](<RICD Tracker Findings Final.pdf>) —
  the final validation findings: the eight validated features, the two
  honest negative results, the governance override, and the full real-data
  result (100.00% leave-one-out accuracy across the 54-institution panel).
- [`RICD Tracker Narrative Final.pdf`](<RICD Tracker Narrative Final.pdf>) —
  the narrative account of how each result was actually reached, including
  the dead ends and corrections along the way, not just the final state.
- [`RICD Tracker Process Log Final.pdf`](<RICD Tracker Process Log Final.pdf>) —
  the consolidated process record: methodological lessons, the panel
  four-category labeling discipline, and the data- and feature-verification
  steps that produced the validated result.
- [`Higher Ed Sector Findings.pdf`](<Higher Ed Sector Findings.pdf>) — a
  separate analysis of what the tracker's results imply about the U.S.
  higher-education sector as a whole, distinct from the validation result
  itself.
- [`Claude's Account of Carmen's Role in Building RICD and the higher-ed tracker.pdf`](<Claude's Account of Carmen's Role in Building RICD and the higher-ed tracker.pdf>)
  and [`ChatGPT's Account of Its own Role in Building FDFM, RICS, and RICD 1.0.pdf`](<ChatGPT's Account of Its own Role in Building FDFM, RICS, and RICD 1.0.pdf>) —
  each AI collaborator's own account of working with Carmen on the
  framework and the tracker, so the real division of labor is checkable
  rather than asserted.
- [`RICD Adapter Instructional Manual.pdf`](<RICD Adapter Instructional Manual.pdf>) —
  an instructional manual for engineers working with the RICD adapter
  contract directly.
- [`Actor Tracker Seed Note.pdf`](<Actor Tracker Seed Note.pdf>) — a seed
  note for a genuinely different kind of tracker planned for later: an
  actor tracker, investigating the specific real decisions and actors
  behind a collapse (board minutes, depositions, investigative findings),
  rather than the aggregate financial and enrollment effects the
  higher-ed tracker measures.
- [`RICD Integration Manifest Complete.pdf`](<RICD Integration Manifest Complete.pdf>) —
  the standing verification record: every mechanism confirmed built into
  the RICD framework, section by section, with the exact manuscript text
  shown for each, kept specifically to be checked against the manuscript
  rather than trusted on its own.
