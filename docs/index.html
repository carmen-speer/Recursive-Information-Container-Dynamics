Live-scored institutions

These institutions were scored automatically from real, current government data by the live pipeline (separate from the 54-institution validation panel above). "Insufficient data" means the pipeline ran but real data for that institution was genuinely incomplete -- not a fabricated result.
What the score means, and what it doesn't. This number is the classifier's posterior estimate that an institution's current observed dynamics sit inside the modeled collapse regime -- not a calibrated probability that the institution will actually close. Those are different claims, and the live pipeline has already surfaced a concrete reason they diverge: frac_high_entropy measures the size of a resource swing, not its direction, so a large positive shock and a large destabilizing one currently look alike to it. University of Houston (87.3% here) received a real $1.3B infusion from the Texas University Fund in the same period this score is drawn from -- financially the opposite of distress -- while University of Phoenix-Arizona (100.0%) is mid-restructuring after a real, large contraction, which this model has no separate "completed reset" state to distinguish from ongoing collapse. Sweet Briar College (52.4%) is the cleanest test case for that same gap: it underwent a real near-closure and recovery in 2015, so its score today is a live check of whether the model can tell "still collapsing" apart from "recovered from a collapse." None of these three numbers should be read as "this institution is 52-100% likely to close" -- they're evidence for exactly the model limitation described in the repository's Known Gaps section, not embarrassing exceptions being explained away. A fix (giving the regime classifier the signed direction of a shock, and a distinct state for a completed institutional reset) is scoped but not yet built or re-validated against the 54-institution panel -- stated here as open, unfixed work, the same way it's stated in the README.
